Concert Hall Cartoon Style Notes
================================

Overall aesthetic
- Clue-inspired theatrical cartoon interior.
- Matte, softly rendered surfaces rather than glossy or photorealistic ones.
- Clean, readable shapes with elegant proportions and simplified detail.
- Warm, rich, upscale mood: refined but still stylized.

Rendering language
- Use smooth digital illustration with crisp outlines or subtle dark edge definition.
- Avoid heavy texture noise, hyperreal reflections, or photographic detail.
- Keep shading simple and controlled: broad gradients, gentle falloff, and minimal micro-detail.
- Favor shape clarity over realism.

Color and lighting
- Warm amber-brown architectural palette.
- Deep burgundy or wine-red curtains with gold trim/tassels.
- Lighting should feel well-lit but not harsh: warm chandelier glow, soft wall-sconce glow, even ambient fill.
- Saturation should be moderate, slightly muted, not neon.


Composition rules for future matching scenes
- Center the main object or room focal point.
- Maintain strong symmetry where possible.
- Keep foreground framing elements (like curtains) clear and intentional.
- Prefer large, legible masses over tiny detail.
- The final image should look like a polished storybook / board-game illustration, not a realistic rendering.

Do / don't
- DO keep it matte, elegant, cartoonish, and clue-like.
- DO use warm theatrical lighting.
- DO simplify forms while preserving the key physical elements.
- DON'T introduce photo textures or extreme realism.
- DON'T make colors overly saturated.
- DON'T clutter the room with too many extra objects.
