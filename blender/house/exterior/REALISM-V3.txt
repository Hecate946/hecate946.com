HECATE946 HOUSE EXTERIOR — REALISM V3

Fixes:
- Existing house objects are now direct-linked from house.blend, forced visible,
  centered, grounded, and scaled to the exterior scene. The build prints
  "HOUSE IMPORT OK" with final bounds.
- PREVIEW now prefers low-sample Cycles + denoising, not Eevee.
- Ground, stone, wood, foliage, hills and snow receive restrained procedural
  variation/bump.
- Tree canopies use many smaller smooth clusters.
- Shrubs use overlapping smooth lobes.
- Walkway is a real constant-width series of stone slabs.
- You can render one season while iterating with HECATE_EXTERIOR_SEASONS=spring.

Recommended first run:
HECATE_EXTERIOR_QUALITY=PREVIEW HECATE_EXTERIOR_SEASONS=spring blender --background --python blender/house/exterior/build_exterior_25d.py
