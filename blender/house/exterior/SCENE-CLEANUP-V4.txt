Changes
-------
1. No more orange overlays.
   - Window glow cards are disabled entirely.
   - The house should now appear as imported from house.blend without facade overlays.

2. Import-only house workflow.
   - The exterior builder no longer regenerates the house.
   - It now requires blender/house/house.blend to already exist.

3. Grass / shrubs / flowers rebuilt.
   - Replaces texture-heavy imported foreground assets with:
     * a geometry-nodes lawn
     * procedural formal shrubs
     * restrained flower groundcover
   - This is meant to reduce or eliminate the GPU texture warnings while also
     giving the foreground a cleaner, more scene-bible-appropriate look.

4. Composition refinement.
   - slightly more open framing
   - trees farther outward / slightly back
   - quieter spring lighting
